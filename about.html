My Story

I am Md. Mizanur Rahaman, a Master's student (Final Year) in Civil Engineering at the University of Tokyo, Japan, where I am a proud recipient of the Asian Development Bank–Japan Scholarship Program (ADB-JSP). My graduate research, carried out within the Construction Management and Infrastructure Systems Laboratory under Associate Professor Pang-jo Chun, focuses on developing AI-enabled frameworks for automated bridge damage detection, severity classification, and BIM-integrated visualization for structural health monitoring.

Prior to my graduate studies, I spent over six years as a structural engineer in Bangladesh, most recently as a Senior Structural Engineer at Sthapona Consultants Limited. During this period I was involved in the analysis and design of reinforced concrete and steel structures, BIM-assisted engineering coordination, retrofitting and structural auditing, and construction quality evaluation — experience that gave me a deep appreciation for the practical challenges facing infrastructure inspection professionals in the field.

It is precisely this intersection of real-world engineering constraints and emerging AI capabilities that motivates my PhD ambitions. Having witnessed first-hand how manual inspection workflows are time-consuming, inconsistent, and limited in scale, I am committed to building data-driven, computer-vision-based tools that make infrastructure safety assessment faster, more objective, and fully integrated with modern BIM and digital twin environments. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities starting in 2027 to continue this research at an international level.
